Our story

Bake O’Clock is a home bakery. There’s no shop counter and no shelf of yesterday’s bakes. Everything is made to order in our kitchen, for a date you choose.

That’s why we ask for 2–3 days’ notice. It gives us time to plan your design and bake it close to your celebration.

Whether it’s a simple chocolate cake or a design you’ve been dreaming about, we treat every order like a gift we’re about to hand over.
